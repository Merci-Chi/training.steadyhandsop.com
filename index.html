Core Frameworks — The Official Glossary

The definitions below are the single source of truth used across every module. Learn them once, apply them everywhere.

CGP — Challenges · Goals · Plans

Explore the buyer's world: the challenges they face, the goals those challenges block, and their current plan.

Discovery

TCI — Timeline · Consequences · Implications

The objective pros & cons: consequences of staying put vs. positive implications of solving it. Never manipulative.

Explore

BAA — Budget · Authority · Agenda

Qualify money, decision power, and the client's priorities & timing. Our ethical successor to legacy BANT.

Qualify

SPIN — Situation · Problem · Implication · Need-payoff

The classic questioning ladder that moves a conversation from facts to felt need.

Questioning

DARE — Define · Analyze · Report · Establish

Our diagnostic engine: define the goal, analyze the state, report the quantified gap, establish the solution.

Diagnosis

LACE — Listen · Acknowledge · Clarify · Execute

The objection-handling loop: hear fully, validate, dig to the real objection, respond with value.

Objections

Challenger — Teach · Tailor · Take Control

Deliver insight the client didn't have, tailored to their world, while confidently guiding the sale.

Insight

Flywheel — Attract · Engage · Deliver · Delight · Amplify

Self-reinforcing growth: the closed deal is the starting line, not the finish.

Growth
