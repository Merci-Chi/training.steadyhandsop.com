Natural Upgrade Pathways

Standard → Backend, Without Pressure

1. LISTEN FOR THE SIGNAL "I change my prices a lot." · "We keep adding services." · "I'd love to show our reviews."
2. CONNECT, DON'T PUSH "With the Backend plan, you'd update those yourself in minutes — want me to show you what that looks like?"
3. LET THEM CHOOSE Present the difference objectively — the TCI mindset. If Standard genuinely fits, say so. That honesty is what makes them trust the next recommendation.

The best upsell moment is often after delivery, when the client feels the friction of requesting every small change. On the first call: mention it once — never twice.

Quick Check

Test Yourself Before Moving On

1. A prospect asks: "So what does the $100 actually cover?"

"Design only — the build is billed separately." "The full design and build of your website. The preview is a starting point — we customize the final site for your business before it goes live." "A basic template — customization costs extra."

This is the official wording — the preview process is your best answer to price skepticism.

2. A salon owner says she changes her service prices almost weekly. Your recommendation:

Standard — it's cheaper, and cheaper always wins Backend — the admin management area is built for content that changes more often; and plans can be changed anytime as needs evolve Neither — refer her to the team immediately

Match plan to content-change frequency. And remember: flexibility removes her fear of choosing.

3. A prospect asks you to also run their Facebook ads. You:

Are honest: "That's outside what I handle personally — let me connect you with our team" via official channels Say "Sure, we can do that" — you can figure it out later Say "We don't do anything digital" — shut it down
