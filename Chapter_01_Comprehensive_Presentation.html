Quick Check

Test Yourself Before Moving On

1. In our TCI framework, what does "C" stand for?

Cost of the solution Consequences — the objective downsides of not solving the problem Context of the client

TCI = Timeline · Consequences · Implications (the positive side of solving it).

2. In BAA, the last "A" is:

Agenda — the client's priorities, timing, and schedule Action Alignment

BAA = Budget · Authority · Agenda — our ethical successor to legacy BANT.

3. A prospect asks: "Why should I pay $100?" According to the Mandate, you should:

Offer a discount immediately List every technical feature of the website Sell the outcome — what a working website does for their business
